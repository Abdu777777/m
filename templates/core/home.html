Education

Build process, not promises.

⌁

Structure & context

A break of structure can support continuation. A change of character may signal weakening momentum, but neither guarantees the next move.

◈

Support & resistance

Treat levels as zones, wait for confirmation, and define invalidation before entry rather than placing stops mechanically at obvious levels.

◎

Trade journal

Record entry, exit, risk in R, rationale and execution quality. Review losing trades as carefully as winning ones.
